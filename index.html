Classes

Move with purpose.

Four distinct class styles, one training ground for character. Whether you're building strength, chasing stillness, or sweating it out — we've built the studio to meet you where you are.

Reformer Pilates

Targeted strength and control on the reformer. Low-impact, high-return — lengthen, tone and restore with every spring.

Hot Mat Pilates

Classical Pilates meets heat. Deep core work in a warmed studio — mobility, focus and sweat in equal measure.

Les Mills

Globally programmed strength, conditioning and cardio. Expertly scaled, music-driven, and designed to push you forward.

Hot Yoga

Breath, heat and release. Flow sequences that build flexibility, calm the mind, and leave you centred.
