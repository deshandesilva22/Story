Our story

Built on the simple idea that movement is better together.

Hourglass began with a question — what if a studio could feel less like a gym and more like a Sunday morning with friends? Today, we host classes, workshops and events that bring our local community closer with every breath.

From sunrise reformer to candle-lit yin, every class is an invitation to slow down, show up, and belong.
