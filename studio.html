Our Manifesto

At Hourglass Studios, we believe discipline builds confidence, and confidence unlocks freedom.

We are more than a Pilates studio — we are a training ground for character. Every class is an opportunity to show up for yourself. Every rep, every breath, every hold is a small act of self-respect. We built this space for people who want to become stronger — in their bodies, in their minds, and in their lives.

01

Discipline

Showing up isn't glamorous — it's repeatable. We design classes that reward consistency and give you the structure to build a practice that compounds.

02

Confidence

Confidence is earned on the mat, one small promise kept at a time. We coach progressions that let you see and feel yourself getting stronger.

03

Freedom

A body you trust is a life with more options. Move with ease, breathe deeper, and carry that freedom into everything you do outside the studio.

Our Classes

Five disciplines, one studio.

Every class is programmed, coached and scaled to meet you wherever you are — beginner or seasoned, we've got a session with your name on it.

Reformer Pilates

Spring-loaded resistance training that lengthens and strengthens in the same breath. Low impact, high return — expect deep core work, postural awareness, and lean tone.

Hot Mat Pilates

Classical mat work in a warmed studio. Muscles move freely, mobility improves, and the heat forces focus — a mental reset with a serious physical payoff.

Les Mills

World-class choreographed programs built by Les Mills — BODYPUMP, BODYCOMBAT and more. Music-driven, coach-led, and guaranteed to move you.

Hot Yoga

Breath-led flows in the heat. Improve flexibility, calm the nervous system, and end every class feeling lighter than you walked in.
