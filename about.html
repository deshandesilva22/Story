Our story

A space built on belonging.

Hourglass Studios was born from a simple question: what if movement felt less like a workout, and more like coming home?

How it began

From a small idea to a big-hearted community.

We opened our doors with a single reformer, a borrowed sound system and a wish — to create the kind of studio we wished existed. A space without intimidation. A class roster shaped by what our community actually wanted.

Years later, we're proud to host thousands of classes a year — but the heart of what we do hasn't changed. People first. Always.

What we stand for

Four things we believe in deeply.

🌱

Community first

Every choice we make starts with “does this serve our members?”

💜

Inclusive always

All bodies, ages, abilities and identities are celebrated here.

🌿

Mindful movement

Strong, sustainable, and rooted in breath — never burnout culture.

✨

Joyful practice

Movement should feel good. We build classes that leave you smiling.

The space

Designed to feel like a deep exhale.

Soft light, plants in every corner, polished timber underfoot, and a community café where post-class conversations happen.

Our reformer studio, mat space and warm yoga room are all built for connection — between teacher, student, and self.
