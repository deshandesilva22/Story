Terms & Conditions

The fine print.

1. Membership

All weekly memberships are paid by direct debit, processed in advance. Memberships are no lock-in — you may cancel at any time by giving seven (7) days' written notice via email or through the Hourglass Studios app.

2. Welcome Pack

The 5 for $60 Welcome Pack is valid for new clients only and expires 14 days from the date of first class activation. Unused classes after 14 days are forfeited. The Welcome Pack is non-transferable and non-refundable.

3. Class Packs

Class packs are valid for 12 months from date of purchase. Unused classes expire after this period. Packs are non-refundable and non-transferable between class types, but classes can be shared with family members subject to studio approval.

4. Bookings & Cancellations

All class bookings must be made through the Momence booking system or the Hourglass Studios app. Cancellations more than 4 hours before class are free of charge. Cancellations within 4 hours, or no-shows, will forfeit the class credit. Repeated no-shows may result in temporary booking restrictions.

5. Late Arrivals

For safety, entry may be refused more than 5 minutes after class start. A forfeited class will apply.

6. Health & Safety

You are responsible for disclosing any medical conditions, injuries, or pregnancy to instructors prior to class. Hourglass Studios accepts no liability for injuries arising from undisclosed conditions or failure to follow instructor guidance. Clients train at their own risk.

7. Studio Conduct

We ask that all members treat staff, instructors and fellow clients with respect. Hourglass Studios reserves the right to refuse service or cancel memberships for inappropriate conduct without refund.

8. Pricing & Changes

Prices are in AUD and include GST. Hourglass Studios reserves the right to amend pricing, timetable, and terms with 14 days' notice via email or in-studio posting.

9. Liability Waiver

By participating in classes at Hourglass Studios, you acknowledge that fitness activity involves inherent risk. You voluntarily assume those risks and release Hourglass Studios, its directors, employees and instructors from liability to the fullest extent permitted by law.

10. Privacy

Personal information is collected and stored in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles and is used only to manage your membership, bookings and communications from the studio. Contact us to access, update or delete your data.
